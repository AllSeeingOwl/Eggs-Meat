# **Eggheads vs. Meatheads: The Master Design Loop** 

**Document Type:** Strategic Summary / Final Concept Review 

**Vision:** A 100% performance-based, asymmetrical chaos-sim that teaches high culture through physical comedy. 

## **1. The Pre-Match: The Lobby & "The Gym/Library"** 

- **The Wait:** Players spawn into an interactive lobby. Eggheads shimmy around "The Library" (practicing typing and trivia), while Meatheads are in "The Gym" (practicing charges and belly-flops). 

- **Matchmaking:** The system maintains a **10:1 Ratio** . A 44-player match consists of 40 Eggheads and 4 Meatheads. 

- **Hardware Detection:** The game detects KBM vs. Controller. KBM players are tagged for "Transcription Tasks"; Controller players for "Pattern/Rhythm Tasks." 

## **2. The Match Loop: The 5-Level Gauntlet** 

Matches are played in a series of rounds. Each round eliminated a percentage of the losing faction or resets the objective.

## **4. The Post-Match: The Reveal & Progression** 

The match ends. **IQ Points** (Eggs) and **Heat** (Meat) are tallied based on performance and community milestones. 

- **The Unboxing:** 

   - **Eggheads:** Enter the "Great Archive" to unlock "The Hair" (Academic prestige/wigs). 

   - **Meatheads:** Enter the "Locker Room Slam" to unlock "The Heel" (Wrestling personas). 

- **No Microtransactions:** 100% of items are earned through mastery. 

- **Season 1 Roadmap:** Progressing through "Gearheads & Great Works" to unlock the community-wide "Great Exhibition" level. 

## **5. Audience & Streaming: "The Grandstand"** 

The game is built for the "Clip Culture." 

- **Sabotage:** Twitch viewers vote on "Category Curveballs" or trigger "Meathead Mutinies." 

- **Emote-Face:** Meatheads' googly eyes reflect chat's favorite emotes. 

- **The Commentary Box:** High-tier players can act as shoutcasters, highlighting the "Summa Cum Laude" performers. 

## **6. The "Brain vs. Brawn" Equilibrium** 

- **Egghead Edge:** Safety in numbers and the ability to use "Yolk Armor" via correct trivia answers. 

- **Meathead Edge:** Raw physical force and the ability to manipulate the environment (e.g., grease/oil spills). 

- **Balance:** Movement is intentionally awkward for both, turning every interaction into a hilarious "clumsy" physics event.
